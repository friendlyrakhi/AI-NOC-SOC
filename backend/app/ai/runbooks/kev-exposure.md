# Known-exploited vulnerability on internet-facing assets

Applies when scanners report a vulnerability listed as known exploited on exposed systems.

Steps:
1. Prioritise internet-facing assets, then those with high criticality.
2. Apply the vendor fix or mitigation within the remediation SLA for known-exploited issues.
3. Search for exploitation indicators on the affected assets before and after patching.
4. Record risk acceptance only with an owner and an expiry date.
